QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

How do I install LUTs in Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve? +
In Premiere, drop the .cube file into a Lumetri Color → Creative → Look slot, or use Lumetri's "Browse" option. In DaVinci, copy the .cube files into your Resolve LUT folder and right-click the Color page → "Update LUTs." After install, every LUT becomes a one-click look.
Will these work with my camera's footage? +
Yes — our LUTs are built for footage shot in standard Rec.709 picture profiles, plus log profiles like S-Log3, V-Log, D-Log, and C-Log. We recommend a normalization step before applying for Log footage to get the most accurate look.
Can I use these for client work and commercial projects? +
Absolutely. Your purchase includes a commercial license for unlimited client and personal projects. The only thing you can't do is resell or redistribute the .cube files themselves.
How do I receive the files after I pay? +
After successful payment, you'll receive a confirmation email with a secure download link to the .cube files. Delivery is automatic and instant. If you don't receive the email within 5 minutes, check your spam folder or email us.
Do you offer refunds? +
Because LUTs are digital files delivered immediately on purchase, all sales are final. If you have an issue installing or using the LUTs, email us — we'll help you get up and running.
