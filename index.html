About Positiva

Positiva Films began on the road — 160,000 kilometres of it. Across highways, ghats, coastlines, and high-altitude passes, we built a body of work rooted in one belief: that India's most compelling stories belong to the people rarely seen on screen. That documentary instinct now drives everything we make — from certified aerial cinematography for the Ministry of Tourism and international brands, to FPV footage that finds angles no conventional drone can reach.
