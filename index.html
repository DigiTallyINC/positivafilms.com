OPEN ROAD INDIA

Where Positiva Films began

“A journey to meet the people who make up the essential fabric of the country. People who we see every day, but rarely meet.”Open Road India · The Reel Real India

Those kilometres up there were driven, not flown. Before Positiva Films, there was Open Road India — a 13-episode documentary series about the country's everyday people: Kumbh foot soldiers in Nasik, kite boys, puppeteers with a purpose, surfers in Puducherry, a cobbler in Amritsar. That instinct — find the person, earn the shot — is what this studio is built on.
