SuperGradeFifteen nodes, collapsed into one.

A single-node cinematic base-grade engine for DaVinci Resolve. A built-in colour-space transform, a film-emulation core, and creative finishing — halation, grain, bloom, split-tone — built from first principles in real colour science. Drop it on one node and grade.

What it does
( 01 )

ONE NODE, THE WHOLE GRADEFrom log to look in a single step

/ CST

Colour-Space Transform

Built-in input and output transforms in DaVinci Wide Gamut / Intermediate. Feed it log from any camera; it lands in the right space — no extra CST nodes.

/ FILM

Film-Emulation Core

A film-trait engine for tone, colour cross-talk, and density — the print-film character that makes digital footage feel shot, not rendered.

/ SKIN

Skin & Primary

Balance, exposure and skin-tone protection tuned in real colour science, so faces hold through the look instead of fighting it.

/ HALATION

Halation & Bloom

Highlight halation and bloom modelled on real lens and film behaviour — warm glow into the highlights without crushing detail.

/ GRAIN

Grain & Texture

Filmic grain and lens-FX finishing that sit under the image, adding texture and motion without the digital-noise look.

/ SPLIT

Split-Tone & Look

Creative split-toning and look controls for the final mood — teal-and-orange, warm cinema, or your own signature, all on the one node.
