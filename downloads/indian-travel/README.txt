  10 Cinematic .cube LUTs

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
- 10 .cube LUT files (industry standard, 33-point cubes)
- This README.txt

The LUTs are designed for Rec.709 footage out of the camera —
either straight-from-camera profiles (Standard, Natural) or
already-converted log footage. They are NOT designed to be
applied directly to raw log; convert to Rec.709 first.


INSTALLATION
------------
The .cube format is universal. Drop these files in once, restart
your editor, and the LUTs become available in every project.

PREMIERE PRO
  1. Window > Lumetri Color > Creative tab.
  2. Click the "Look" dropdown > Browse...
  3. Select any .cube file from this pack.
  Tip: For permanent installs, copy the .cube files to:
       Mac:     /Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/LUTs/Creative/
       Windows: C:\Program Files\Adobe\Common\LUTs\Creative\

DAVINCI RESOLVE
  1. Project Settings > Color Management > Lookup Tables.
  2. Click "Open LUT Folder", drop the .cube files in, click "Update Lists".
  3. In the Color page, right-click any node > LUT > [find your pack].

FINAL CUT PRO
  1. Apply the "Custom LUT" effect to your clip from the Effects browser.
  2. In the inspector, click the LUT dropdown > Choose Custom LUT.
  3. Select any .cube file from this pack.

AFTER EFFECTS
  1. Apply Effect > Utility > Apply Color LUT.
  2. Choose a .cube file from this pack.

PHOTOSHOP
  1. Layer > New Adjustment Layer > Color Lookup.
  2. In the properties, "Load 3D LUT..." > select a .cube file.

LIGHTROOM CLASSIC
  Lightroom does not natively support .cube. Use the Adobe DNG
  Profile Editor or a third-party plug-in such as 3D LUT Creator
  to convert .cube to .xmp profiles.


HOW TO USE THESE LUTs
---------------------
1. White-balance and expose your shot first. LUTs are a finishing
   tool, not a fix for raw correction.
2. Apply the LUT.
3. Lower the LUT intensity / opacity to taste — most travel looks
   sit beautifully at 60-85% on real footage.
4. Layer with subtle grain, light contrast tweaks, and skin-tone
   protection for the most cinematic finish.

For HDR or Rec.2020 timelines, these LUTs may need transform
nodes.
